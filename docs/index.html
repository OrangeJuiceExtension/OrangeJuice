The useful bits.

Small additions for the way you already use Hacker News.

Stay in the conversation

Read a comment. Select a line. Reply right there. Orange Juice keeps the thread in view while you write.

Find the new comments

Come back to a thread and see which comments arrived while you were away.

Give your mouse a break

Move with j and k, reply with r, or save a favorite with f. Press ? to see the shortcuts and change the bindings.

Get to know the person behind a comment

Hover over a name to see their profile. Follow people you want to hear from, or mute the ones you don’t.

Share a link to the conversation

Copy a story or comment’s Hacker News URL with a keyboard shortcut.

Read the code. See the diagram.

Code is easier to read, and Mermaid diagrams render right inside comments. Emoji shortcodes work, too.

Keep up with your people

A following feed collects recent comments and submissions from the HN users you follow. Reorder the list and refresh each person when you want.
